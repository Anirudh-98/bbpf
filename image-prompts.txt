1. READ THIS FIRST - HONEST USE OF AI IMAGES
==============================================================================
BPPF asks people for money, so how the pictures are presented matters.

 - AI photos are fine as atmosphere and illustration. They are NOT evidence.
   Do not caption them as BPPF's actual villages, farmers or beneficiaries.
 - Never use AI people for testimonials, "stories from the field", leadership
   photos or impact claims. Those need real, consented photographs.
   (The homepage testimonial slider was removed for exactly this reason.)
 - Real photos beat AI photos. Every entry below doubles as a SHOT LIST: hand
   it to a local photographer and shoot the real thing in a BPPF village at
   the light described. Swap real photos in over time; keep the file names.
 - Consider one line in the footer: "Some images are illustrative."
 - Never generate the face of a real, named person.
 - Show dignity: work, skill, pride, learning, abundance. No pity poses.

2. THE LOOK: ATTRACTIVE AND REAL
==============================================================================
Think of a National Geographic story or a premium NGO annual report: real
places, real people, a skilled photographer, the best light of the day.
Six things make a picture both attractive and believable:

 1. LIGHT does most of the work.
    Sunrise, golden hour, backlight with a glowing rim on hair and clothes,
    dust and mist catching the sun, soft window or shade light indoors.
    Never flat grey midday light. Every prompt below asks for good light.

 2. COMPOSITION - one clear focal point, in layers.
    Foreground (flowers, grass, a pot) -> subject -> background (village,
    hills). Use leading lines: field bunds, lanes, crop rows, a dam wall.
    Leave calm space where the website puts text.

 3. COLOUR - rich but natural.
    Warm red-ochre soil, deep greens, blue-teal sky, and saturated saree
    colours (marigold yellow, magenta, teal, indigo). A gentle warm grade.
    Never neon greens, never HDR halos, never a sepia filter.

 4. PEOPLE - warm, genuine, dignified.
    Natural laughter, quiet pride, absorbed concentration. Healthy, bright
    cotton clothes with honest wear. Mid-action and candid. They do not stare
    at the camera (except the one portrait). No stock-photo smiles.

 5. MOOD - hopeful and abundant.
    Green, water, harvest, learning, community. Alive and proud, never
    poverty.

 6. REAL - photographic language and natural texture.
    A real camera, lens and aperture in the prompt. Natural skin with pores
    and lines, not airbrushed. A believable amount of dust, mud and creases.

WORDS TO AVOID (they create the plastic, over-processed "AI look"):
  ultra detailed, 8K, hyperrealistic, masterpiece, HDR, perfect, trending on
  artstation, octane, unreal engine, "cinematic 4k render".

WORDS THAT HELP (already inside every prompt):
  professional editorial documentary photograph, golden hour, backlit, rim
  light, shallow depth of field, Kodak Portra 400 colour, fine film grain,
  candid, well composed, clear focal point.

WORKFLOW
  a) Generate 6-8 variants of each prompt. Choose by asking: "would a real
     photographer have been standing there?" Reject any that look glossy or
     too perfect; keep the one with natural light and natural faces.
  b) Zoom to 100% and check the usual tells:
       HANDS      keep them busy or half-hidden; count fingers; inpaint fixes
       FACES      no cloned faces in groups; turn some people away from camera
       EYES/SMILE natural laughter, not a frozen grin; eyes look at the task
       CLOTHES    saree pleats and dhoti wraps are often wrong - zoom in
       TOOLS/POTS spade, plough, sickle, pots must make physical sense
       TEXT       invented scripts on signs look fake - leave signs blank
       LIGHT      shadows must all fall the same way; sky must not glow
       COLOUR     greens should be lush but natural, never neon
  c) Finish (5 minutes in any photo editor):
       auto-tone -> gentle S-curve contrast -> warm the highlights slightly
       -> vibrance +8 (not saturation) -> keep skin tones natural ->
       brighten faces a touch (+0.3 EV) -> fine grain ~3% -> faint vignette
       -> light sharpen -> export sRGB JPEG quality ~85.
  d) File size: keep each image under ~800 KB (hero poster under 250 KB).
     The website converts to WebP/AVIF automatically.
  e) Fix a bad detail by inpainting that region only. Do not regenerate the
     whole picture for one wrong hand.

QUICK FIXES WHEN A RESULT IS OFF
  Looks flat or dull      add "low golden sun behind the subject, glowing rim
                          light, soft warm haze"
  Looks fake or plasticky lower stylize (Midjourney --stylize 30-50) and add
                          "Kodak Portra 400, fine film grain, natural skin
                          texture with pores"
  Faces look stiff        add "laughing mid-sentence, looking at a task, not
                          at the camera"
  Colours look garish     add "natural colour, gentle warm grade" and remove
                          any colour words you added yourself
  Scene looks staged      add "candid, people busy with real work, ordinary
                          background clutter"
  Too clean               add a little "dust in the air, soil on clothes"

3. STYLE BLOCK + NEGATIVE PROMPT
==============================================================================
STYLE BLOCK (already inside every prompt; reuse it for any extra shot):
  Professional editorial documentary photograph, National Geographic quality,
  shot on a full-frame camera with a prime lens, beautiful natural light,
  rich natural colours with a gentle warm grade, Kodak Portra 400 look, fine
  film grain, natural skin texture, genuine warm expressions, candid but
  beautifully composed, clear focal point, no text, no watermark.

NEGATIVE PROMPT (paste into the "negative prompt" field; in Midjourney put it
after --no):
  watermark, text, captions, logo, readable signage, cartoon, illustration,
  painting, 3D render, CGI, plastic skin, airbrushed, over-smoothed, glossy,
  HDR halos, oversaturated, neon colours, sepia filter, fantasy lighting,
  lens flare, bokeh balls, extra fingers, deformed hands, distorted faces,
  cloned faces, identical people, frozen grin, stock-photo smile, staring
  into camera, western clothing, modern skyscrapers, tourist stereotypes,
  snake charmers, elephants, palaces, costume turbans, sad or begging poses,
  poverty stereotypes, torn dirty clothes, dull flat lighting, grey washed-out
  look, muddy colours, harsh midday sun, cluttered composition, sterile
  staged set

TOOL NOTES (check your tool's current parameters, they change often)
  Midjourney : append  --ar 3:2  (or the ratio in each entry)  --style raw
               --stylize 50   (lower stylize = less "AI polish")
  Flux / Imagen / Gemini / Ideogram / Firefly : set the aspect ratio in the
               interface; plain-English prompts work as written.
  Ask for the size listed in each entry, or upscale to it afterwards.

4. REFERENCE BANK - REAL DETAILS OF AN INDIAN (TELANGANA) VILLAGE
==============================================================================
Sprinkle any of these into a prompt when a scene looks generic.

PEOPLE AND CLOTHES
  Women : cotton sarees in marigold yellow, magenta, teal, indigo, maroon
          (pallu over the shoulder, or tucked at the waist while working),
          thin borders, glass bangles, small bindi, toe rings, oiled braids,
          jasmine in the hair.
  Men   : white or checked cotton shirts, lungi (dhoti for elders), a white
          cotton towel over the shoulder, a cotton head cloth for field work,
          worn rubber slippers.
  Kids  : navy-and-white school uniforms, two braids with ribbons, cloth bags.
  Skin  : a natural range of brown tones, with pores and lines.

HOUSES AND STREET
  Whitewashed or ochre-painted brick and lime houses, red country-tile roofs,
  flat concrete roofs, the odd thatched roof, low compound walls, rice-flour
  muggu patterns at the doorstep, a rope cot outside, a tulasi plant pedestal,
  hand pump or borewell, satellite dish, laundry line, bicycles, old
  motorbikes, tractor with trolley, a small temple with a saffron flag.

LAND AND ANIMALS
  Red and black cotton soil, granite boulders and low rocky hills, tamarind,
  neem, banyan, mango and palm trees, cattle egrets, village tanks (chervu)
  with reeds and lotus. Crops: paddy, cotton, chilli, maize, jowar (sorghum),
  bajra (pearl millet), groundnut, turmeric, marigold borders. Animals: white
  Ongole bulls, buffaloes, goats, country hens, desi cows.

OBJECTS
  Steel and brass pots, steel tumblers, plastic pots (kudam), bamboo winnowing
  trays and baskets, wooden plough, sickle, spade, jute sacks, blue plastic
  drums, tarpaulin sheets, whitewashed government buildings with a coloured
  band along the wall.

SEASON AND LIGHT
  Dry season: red dust, cracked earth, glowing in low sun. After monsoon: deep
  greens, bright clouds. Early morning mist. Golden hour. The prompts below
  vary the time of day and the mood so the site does not feel like one long
  sunset.
