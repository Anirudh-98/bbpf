## PART A — DESIGN SYSTEM (from the reference video, re-skinned for BPPF)

**Layout grammar** (copied from the video):
- A content "card" that floats over a soft, blurred nature photo — not a hard-edged full-bleed image.
- A pill-shaped nav bar with a rounded active-state pill.
- Small dot-eyebrow label above every section headline (e.g. `● About Agrovia`).
- Two-line headlines: line 1 in a bold grotesk sans, line 2 (the emotional word) in an **italic serif**.
- Numbered/plus-sign accordions for FAQs; soft-shadow cards for pillars/services; a horizontal card carousel for testimonials/stories.
- Pill-shaped CTA buttons, one solid (primary) + one outline (secondary), always paired.

**BPPF color palette** (swapping the video's fintech-green for BPPF's own identity — deep-rooted, Indian, trust-first):

| Role | Color | Hex (suggested) |
|---|---|---|
| Primary / Headlines / Nav | Deep Forest Green | `#1B3B2A` |
| Accent / Primary CTA | Terracotta / Saffron | `#C1622D` |
| Secondary Accent | Muted Gold | `#C9A227` |
| Background | Warm Ivory | `#F8F4EC` |
| Card surface | Off-white | `#FFFFFF` |

*(Terracotta and gold echo the coral-red heading and gold badge already on BPPF's investment sheet and logo — so the new site feels like the same organization, not a rebrand.)*

**Typography:**
- Display/serif (for italic accent words): **Fraunces** or **Playfair Display**
- Sans (headlines + body): **Manrope** or **General Sans**
- All headline patterns follow: `Bold Sans line 1` + `Italic Serif accent line 2` — e.g. "Restoring Villages. Reviving *Nature*."

**Recurring components to build once, reuse everywhere:** Eyebrow tag, Two-line headline, Pill button pair, Icon-list row (with expand/collapse), Stat counter block, Photo-caption card, Accordion (FAQ/steps), Horizontal story carousel, Leadership card, Footer with donation block.

### 3.2 How We Work — The 6-Step Model
(Same 6 steps as Home Section 4, but expanded here with 2–3 sentences each.)

**STEP 01 — Understand the Village**
Every village has its own geography, water table, agricultural practices, social structure and challenges. We begin by studying the local context before proposing any intervention.

**STEP 02 — Restore the Natural Foundation**
Focus on soil health, water sources, tree cover and biodiversity — the physical base every other outcome depends on.

**STEP 03 — Strengthen Agriculture**
Support farmers transitioning from chemical to natural farming, improving both yield quality and long-term soil health.

**STEP 04 — Strengthen People**
Invest in education and health — because a resilient village needs capable, healthy people as much as it needs healthy land.

**STEP 05 — Strengthen Livelihoods**
Build economic resilience through productive, sustainable agricultural activity that supports families directly.

**STEP 06 — Build Self-Sustainability**
The goal is not permanent dependence on outside support — it's a village increasingly capable of sustaining its own people, resources and future.

### 6.2 The Village Sustainability Model (economics — from the investment PDF)

**Eyebrow:** ● How It Works

**Headline:** The Village Sustainability *Model*

> **Important framing note for BPPF (carry this caution into the actual site copy, don't skip it):** the source document expresses this as an "Investment Plan" with a stated "return on investment." Presented this way on a public website, it reads as a financial/investment product, which raises regulatory and credibility questions for a Section 8 nonprofit. Recommend reframing publicly as a **"Village Sponsorship / Adoption Contribution"** with transparent use-of-funds reporting, rather than a promised financial return — and getting this page approved by BPPF's own compliance/legal review before publishing figures. The numbers below are reproduced exactly as supplied, for internal use, not as final public copy.

**As supplied:**
- Investment for one village: **₹10,00,000 (₹10 lakh)**
- Manufacturing cost of product: **₹4,000/acre**
- Selling cost of product: **₹6,900/acre**
- Crop-stage revenue progression: Crop 1 – ₹17,25,000 · Crop 2 – ₹29,75,000 · Crop 3 – ₹51,32,950 · Crop 4 – ₹88,54,300 · Crop 5 – ₹1,52,73,700 · Crop 6 – ₹2,63,47,200
- Contributor return: Principal ₹10,00,000 + Profit ₹20,00,000 = **Total ₹30,00,000**
- Expenditure: Salaries, transport, GST, HO expenditure and miscellaneous — **₹1,20,00,000**
- Net after contributor return + expenditure: **₹1,13,00,000**, directed to:
  - Basic Amenities
  - Agriculture
  - Health
  - Education
  - Culture of the village

**Public-facing framing (suggested rewrite for the actual page, once legally reviewed):**
